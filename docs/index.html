FamChat

A simple, private chat for the people on your home Wi-Fi. No accounts, no phone numbers, no servers — it just works on your network.

Download
First time opening it on a Mac? Because FamChat isn't signed with Apple yet, macOS shows an “unidentified developer” warning the first time. Just right-click the FamChat app → Open, then click Open in the dialog. You only do this once. (Windows shows a similar one-time “More info → Run anyway”.)
1

One person starts

Open FamChat, hit “+”, pick a family word, and share the address it gives you.

2

Everyone joins

The others choose “Join,” paste that address, and type the same family word.

3

Chat

You’re in — messages, photos, and files stay on your home network.
